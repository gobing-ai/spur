# M5: Teams declared by role: a member is a role plus an executor

## Goal
A team member is declared as **a role plus an executor**, and either half may be omitted: name the
role and the roster resolves an executor through the tier ladder; name the executor and the role
still records what that member is for.

Feature B2's batch 1 adds an optional `role` to `agent.team[].members[]` (task 0538) so that a
materialized spec carries it. That is plumbing. This feature makes the role the **primary axis** of a
roster, which is what turns Teams from "a list of executor names" into "a team with jobs".

Today a member is `{ executor, purpose?, id? }` (`packages/config/src/index.ts:177-198`) where
`purpose` is free prose — `"Lead — planning & review"` in this repo's own config. Prose cannot be
routed on, validated, or displayed consistently. `role` is the typed field that replaces it as the
machine-readable axis, with `purpose` demoted to human annotation.
## Scope
- In:
    - `role` as the primary declared axis of `agent.team[].members[]`, with `executor` optional when
      a role is given.
    - Resolving a role-only member to a concrete executor through the existing tier ladder, and
      recording the resolution on the materialized spec so it is inspectable.
    - `purpose` demoted to human annotation: still accepted, no longer the identity or routing signal.
    - Surfacing the role wherever a roster is already rendered — `spur team status`,
      `spur agent list --specs`, and the Teams Board roster.
    - Validation: a role must be one of the four in `plugins/sp/references/roles.md`; a member must
      declare at least one of role or executor.
- Out:
    - Addressing a team member **by role**. Two members can share a role, and G4 rejects non-unique
      addressing (`packages/app/src/services/agent-service.ts:1341-1343`). Specs stay addressed by id.
    - The role vocabulary itself, `--agent`, `--spec`, and `agent.default` — feature B2, batch 1.
    - New Teams Board layout or interaction work — features M3 / J4 own the surfaces; this feature
      only adds the role to what they already render.
    - Auto-composing a roster from a workload ("give me a team for this feature"). Interesting, and
      out of scope until role-declared rosters have been used in anger.
    - Cost or routing telemetry — feature J6.

## Notes
### Sequencing

Blocked on feature B2 task 0538, which adds the optional `role` field this feature promotes to
primary. Starting earlier means guessing at the field's shape.

### Why role does not become an address

Recorded so it is not re-proposed. `--agent <role>` selects *a capability*; addressing selects *an
occupant*. Two members can share a role, so a role is not a unique address — the same multiplicity
argument G4 already applied to coding-agent kind
(`packages/app/src/services/agent-service.ts:1341-1343`,
`occupant_lookup_kind_rejected`). Specs stay addressed by id via `--spec` (feature B2, task 0542).

### Deferred to batch 3

- **Auto-composing a roster from a workload.** "Give me a team for feature X" is the obvious next
  step once rosters are role-declared, but it should wait until role-declared rosters have actually
  been used.
- **Role-aware supervision policy.** Whether a failing `reviewer` should be restarted differently
  from a failing `coder` is unexamined.
