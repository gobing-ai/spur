# D8: Proportional workflow upgrade strategy

## Goal

**Destination.** Produce an operator-approved, task-ready workflow upgrade strategy that first restores the correctness of the existing execution contract, then measurably reduces human attention, premium-token use, wall-clock time, and maintenance complexity while preserving proportional, evidence-backed safety.

### What reaching the end looks like

- The workflow ADR set is reconciled against shipped code, tests, docs, and real-run evidence; stale or conflicting decisions have explicit keep, amend, supersede, retire, or implementation-gap dispositions.
- Proven runtime, resume, resolution, security, observability, and evidence-accounting defects are assigned to minimal root-cause stabilization slices before proportional routing is attempted.
- Every shipped workflow and execution surface has a deployment role, fit verdict, and target disposition: keep, simplify/optimize, demote to a procedure or fixture, or retire.
- Gate policy is proportional to deterministic risk and uncertainty, with the routing reason visible in existing run evidence.
- PASS-changing waivers are distinguished from non-waiving reference snapshots; each mechanism has an explicit keep, simplify, replace, or remove recommendation.
- A real, used surrounding workflow is prototyped first through the actual engine, before any proposal changes the canonical task pipeline.
- The final strategy contains measurable budgets or explicit evidence gaps, ADR amendments, rollback boundaries, task-ready implementation slices, and a recorded operator disposition.

## Scope

**In scope**

- ADRs 051, 060, 065, 069-072, 076, 087, 093-100, and 102, plus the baseline chain in ADRs 050, 062, 088, 090, and 092 where it changes workflow or gate behavior.
- All repository, bundled, installed, and project-local workflow definitions and their actual callers.
- Every execution and read surface: engine run/validate/show/list/continue, lifecycle adapters, progress projection, the prompt-level inline driver, and its record-only journal.
- Existing `sp` plugin commands/skills/scripts and `spur` CLI/application/built-in owners used by workflows, including the four script surfaces and portable entrypoint contracts.
- Real-run cost, latency, failure, retry, HITL, proof, correction, and verified-outcome evidence; unknown or unbound measurements remain unknown.
- Proportional gate profiles, workflow fit, baseline semantics, transparent routing, optional workflow-version observation, and surrounding-workflow-first migration.

**Out of scope**

- Implementing the final workflow redesign during wayfinding; this map ends at an approved, task-ready strategy.
- Merging Workspace, Inbox, and Teams into Project; that is a separate product and information-architecture effort.
- Replacing `@gobing-ai/ts-dual-workflow-engine`, adding a generalized workflow/policy DSL, or building a version registry before version changes behavior.
- Weakening trust-boundary validation, path confinement, secret handling, data-loss prevention, accessibility, or explicit operator requirements.
- Adding or changing a public `spur` noun or verb without a later, explicit ADR-051 consent decision.

### Standing principles

- Correctness and verified outcome form the floor; then optimize human attention, fresh premium tokens per verified PASS, wall-clock, and maintenance complexity.
- Unknown evidence remains unknown. A field, baseline, timeout, proof binding, or log is not a safety control until an executable check proves its effect.
- Every route, skip, escalation, waiver, retry, and failure is visible with a bounded reason in existing evidence surfaces.
- Surrounding workflows first; the canonical `task-pipeline` is the last migration target.
- Prefer deletion, demotion, and reuse of existing engine/CLI/application owners over new workflow machinery.

### Decisions so far

- D8 ends at a reviewed strategy plus an explicit operator disposition; approval freezes the strategy and permits later implementation-task creation, while rejection keeps D8 open for revision.
- Proven correctness and evidence defects are prerequisite stabilization slices, not optional optimization work.
- The optional root `version` is initially a quoted, non-empty, behavior-neutral opaque string tag. Absence means `unversioned`; a present literal remains observable beside the exact definition digest. Unknown literals are not silently reclassified, but no supported-version registry or semantic-version parser is added without behavior to dispatch.
- Project/Workspace/Inbox/Teams consolidation remains separate. No public CLI change is implied.

### Open operator decisions

1. Whether composition and corpus baselines survive at all after their real gate effects and maintenance costs are measured.
2. Whether the prompt-level inline interpreter has enough measured benefit to justify executable parity with the engine; default recommendation is to retire it and retain only the cheap implementation-only route.
3. The final proportional risk bands and independent-review floor.
4. Any public-surface change and the eventual budgets, after evidence coverage is sufficient.

### Out of scope

- A second workflow engine, generalized policy DSL, version registry, model judge, or parallel canonical task pipeline.
- Automatic baseline regeneration that silently accepts new debt.
- Making workflow `version` mandatory in the current major release.
- Unrelated working-tree edits and Workspace/Inbox/Teams consolidation.
