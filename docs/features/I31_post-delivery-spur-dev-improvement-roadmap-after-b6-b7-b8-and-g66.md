# I31: Post-delivery spur-dev improvement roadmap after B6 B7 B8 and G66

## Goal

Produce an evidence-backed, operator-reviewable program plan for improving the complete spur-dev workflow after B6, B7, B8, and G66: rank candidate investigations, identify existing ownership, and graduate only evidence-supported implementation slices.

## Scope

**In scope:**

- A post-delivery inventory of the live `spur agent` / `spur message` surfaces, `plugins/sp`, installed adapters, and `config/workflows` contracts that affect complete spur-dev runs.
- Evidence-based candidate ranking for reliability, less operator intervention, usage/quota correctness, workflow economy, CLI journeys, session hygiene, capability visibility, and history feedback.
- Exactly three executable investigation tickets: contract/adoption inventory, usage-to-availability validation, and complete-run reliability/cost measurement.
- Reuse of existing owners and evidence tools, including I7 for semantic drift, P for dispatch reliability, D62 for workflow economy, and existing history/run trace tooling.

**Out of scope:**

- Source, plugin, workflow YAML, `.spur/config.yaml`, or `~/.config/spur/config.yaml` edits during charting.
- New public `spur` verbs, compatibility removals, quota schedulers, automatic retries, or automatic status/feature closure.
- Implementing, resolving, or claiming the investigation tickets in this charting session.

## Acceptance Criteria

This feature is a wayfinder map (`wayfinder-map` tag), not a specified deliverable. It carries no Gherkin acceptance criteria by design: charting records candidates and bounded investigations only. Implementation acceptance criteria are authored after evidence review and explicit operator approval; no such approval is recorded here.

### Evidence and standing constraints

- B6/B7/B8/G66 are treated as delivered inputs only; this map does not re-verify their completion or claim a new implementation.
- The local usage preview completed with exit 0 and no config writes. Its sanitized gist is sufficient for charting: healthy no-op rows included `codex-sol`, `claude`, `pi-zai`, `minimax`, `pi-k3`, `pi-deepseek`, and `grok`; unmapped providers included `cursor`, `antigravity`, `vertexai`, `ollama`, and `zed`; provider errors and no-usage cases remain evidence dimensions. Do not persist auth diagnostic bodies.
- The no-usage selection behavior and the loop's all-healthy mapping behavior remain hypotheses to reproduce against fixtures; they are not recorded as confirmed bugs.
- The first two investigations are independent. The reliability/cost investigation depends on the first inventory to choose representative scenarios; its output can inform the final plan without wiring implementation work into the chart.

### Open questions

- What CLI compatibility and deprecation tolerance is acceptable for any future `spur agent` / `spur message` simplification? This controls whether a candidate can remove or rename existing syntax.
- What quota refresh cadence is acceptable, and should refresh remain manual or be considered for external scheduling? This blocks any scheduling proposal.
- What unattended cost and retry budgets are acceptable for complete-run recovery? This controls the measurement thresholds and any later bounded recovery policy.

### Decisions so far

- The evidence standard is the current decision: hypotheses stay hypotheses until a ticket reproduces them with source-local or sanitized fixture evidence.
- Reliability and less operator intervention are the recommended ordering, not an operator-approved commitment.
- Existing ownership is preserved during charting: I7 for semantic drift, P for dispatch reliability, D62 for workflow economy, and E6 for run/session/cost correlation.
- No new public verbs, source edits, workflow edits, config writes, scheduling, automatic retries, ticket resolution, or feature auto-closure are authorized by this map.

### Not yet specified

- The complete-run denominator, representative provider/fleet cohort, and minimum evidence threshold for calling a reliability or cost pattern actionable.
- The exact adapter targets and version/attestation source that should be treated as installed parity after the contract inventory.
- Which user-journey simplifications belong in the CLI, plugin guidance, or workflow composition once identity and idempotency evidence is collected.
- The final implementation slices, acceptance criteria, and operator-approved priority after the three investigation artifacts are reviewed.

### Out of scope

- Changing `spur agent` or `spur message` verbs/flags, adding compatibility shims, or updating public-surface consent records.
- Editing `plugins/sp`, `config/workflows`, `.spur/config.yaml`, `~/.config/spur/config.yaml`, application source, tests, or generated adapters during this chart.
- Building a quota scheduler, changing provider credentials/auth diagnostics, adding a new usage producer, or introducing automatic retries for unknown outcomes.
- Resolving or closing the child tickets, auto-closing D62 or other referenced features, and declaring any candidate implemented.
