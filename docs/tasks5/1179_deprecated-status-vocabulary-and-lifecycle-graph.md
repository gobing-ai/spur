### Design

**Approach.** Additive enum value + graph edges; every consumer that reads `FEATURE_STATUSES` or `z.enum(FEATURE_STATUSES)` follows automatically. Why: status is frontmatter-only (no DB column), and ADR-135 already validates every transport against the graph, so the graph is the single place the new edges need to exist.

**Frozen names.** Status value `deprecated`; domain icon `🗄️`; alias map identity entry only; workflow `version: "3"`; transition descriptions "Deprecate feature (frozen, retained)", "Un-deprecate (mandatory History entry)", "Remove deprecated feature". No new API, flag or verb.

**Files.** `packages/domain/src/planning/schema.ts` (enum, icons, aliases); `config/workflows/feature-lifecycle.yaml` (state + 7 edges); generated `apps/cli/config/workflows/feature-lifecycle.yaml`; tests `packages/domain/tests/planning-schema.test.ts:241`, `packages/contracts/tests/contract.test.ts:440`, `apps/web/tests/modules/features/components.test.tsx:361`, plus transition coverage in `packages/app/tests/services/feature-transition.test.ts`.

**Anti-patterns.** Do not add `deprecated` to `terminalStates` (kills un-deprecate). Do not add synonyms (`obsolete`, `retired`). Do not add `cancelled→deprecated`. Do not hand-edit the generated `apps/cli/config` copy or `apps/desktop/resources/**`. Do not special-case deprecated in `feature-transition.ts` — the graph owns legality.

**Handoff.** 1180 relies on `FeatureStatus` including `deprecated` and on the graph refusing other exits; 1181 relies on the enum for its filter and on deprecated→active/cancelled for its actions.
